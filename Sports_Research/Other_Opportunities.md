## SECTION 1 — Mexican Government Programs (The Foundation Layer)

### 1.1 Beca Bienestar Benito Juárez
The economic floor every athlete should claim first. Stackable with ALL other programs.

| Level | Amount/month | Eligibility |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Básica (primaria/secundaria) | MXN $840 | Public school enrolled; indigenous priority |
| Media Superior (bachillerato) | MXN $900 | Any public bachillerato |
| Superior (universidad) | MXN $2,400 | Public university enrolled |

**Not sport-specific — but frees household income to enable sport participation. Apply at: becasbenitojuarez.mx**

---

### 1.2 CONADE — The National Sport Ladder

**SINADE Beca Levels (CONADE's scholarship system):**

| Level | Profile | Monthly Stipend |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| Nivel I — Talento | State-level performer, identified talent | MXN $2,000–$4,000 |
| Nivel II — Desarrollo | National medalists; U-age selections | MXN $5,000–$9,000 |
| Nivel III — Alto Rendimiento | Pan-Am / World Championship qualifiers | MXN $10,000–$18,000 |
| Nivel IV — Élite | Olympic/World medalists | MXN $20,000–$30,000+ |

**Entry pathway:** Community → IDERV/CODEH → Juegos Estatales → Federación Nacional → SINADE nomination

**Additional CONADE programs:**
- **PADAR:** Direct support for competition travel and equipment (federation applies, not individual)
- **CNAR (Centro Nacional de Alto Rendimiento, CDMX):** Full residency for SINADE Nivel II+ athletes — world-class facilities, sport science, housing, meals
- **CONADEPI (Indigenous Sport Commission):** Organizes Juegos Deportivos Nacionales Indígenas; direct pathway for indigenous athletes to gain institutional visibility

---

### 1.3 State Institutes (Most Direct Entry Point)

**IDERV (Veracruz):** veracruz.gob.mx/iderv
- Training centers in Xalapa, Orizaba, Córdoba, Veracruz city
- Sports: boxing, wrestling, weightlifting, judo, cycling, athletics
- State beca: MXN $1,500–$3,500/month for state-selection athletes

**CODEH (Hidalgo):** codeh.hidalgo.gob.mx
- CAR Hidalgo in Pachuca (altitude 2,400m — free altitude training)
- Strong wrestling tradition; Hidalgo has produced national-level wrestlers
- State beca: MXN $1,500–$4,000/month

**Both institutes have direct walk-in access.** A coach or community representative can present with an athlete and competition records.

---

### 1.4 INPI — Instituto Nacional de los Pueblos Indígenas

- **Albergues Escolares Indígenas:** Boarding schools with room + board + education for indigenous youth; some near city sport facilities
- **Apoyos para la Educación:** MXN $1,000–$2,500/month for secondary/upper secondary enrollment
- **Document assistance:** INPI helps obtain actas de nacimiento, CURP — bureaucratic barriers that block many athletes from accessing state programs
- **Combinable with:** Everything. INPI is welfare support, not sport funding — no conflict.

---

### 1.5 SEDENA — The Most Underused Pathway ⭐

The **Mexican Army military athlete program** is chronically overlooked and may offer the best total package for an 18–22 year old elite athlete without international results yet.

**What SEDENA offers a "Soldado Deportista":**
- Base salary: MXN $6,800–$9,200/month
- Food + housing allowance: ~MXN $2,700/month
- Full military medical coverage
- **Total package: MXN $9,500–$12,000/month** — the highest of any option accessible without international results
- International competition through **CISM (Conseil International du Sport Militaire)** — military world games including wrestling, boxing, judo, athletics, weightlifting, cycling

**How to access:** Present to nearest Zona Militar with competition records
- Veracruz: 11ª Zona Militar, Jalapa
- Hidalgo: 20ª Zona Militar, Pachuca area

**Age window:** 18–22. Minimum education: secondary school completion.
**Cultural note:** Indigenous youth from Veracruz and Hidalgo enlist in the Mexican military at above-average rates — this pathway has genuine community precedent and familiarity.

## SECTION 2 — Mexican Universities

### The Optimal Stack for a University-Enrolled Athlete

| Source | Monthly Amount |
|--------|---------------|
| Beca Bienestar Superior | MXN $2,400 |
| UV or UAEH Athletic Scholarship | MXN $1,500–$3,500 |
| IDERV/CODEH State Sport Beca | MXN $1,500–$3,500 |
| **Total possible** | **MXN $5,400–$9,400/month** |

Sufficient to live and train in Xalapa or Pachuca.

### Universidad Veracruzana (UV) — Best Option for VZ Athletes
- Intercultural campuses specifically targeting indigenous communities in Veracruz
- Coordinates directly with IDERV
- Campus in Xalapa (most relevant), Orizaba, Veracruz city
- Indigenous student tutoring program supplements athletic support
- Athletic scholarship: MXN $1,500–$4,000/month

### UAEH (Hidalgo) — Best Option for HGO Athletes
- Strong wrestling tradition; Hidalgo has produced national wrestlers
- Pachuca campus at 2,400m altitude (endurance sport advantage)
- Direct connection to CODEH
- Explicit indigenous community outreach
- Athletic scholarship: MXN $1,500–$3,500/month

### UNAM / IPN (CDMX) — Higher Ceiling, Higher Barrier
- World-class sport facilities; strongest scholarship amounts
- Requires relocation to Mexico City
- Competitive academic admission
- Indigenous student support programs exist but competition is higher

### Key Competition: Universiada Nacional (CONDDE)
The national university games. Outstanding performers get flagged by national federations for SINADE and Pan-American competition pathways. **Medaling at Universiada Nacional is a career-accelerating event.**

## SECTION 3 — International Federations (Free + Structured)

### UWW (Wrestling) — Best International Federation Program
- **UWW Academy, Tashkent, Uzbekistan:** Sponsored residency stays for developing-nation athletes; world-class coaching; application through Federación Mexicana de Lucha
- **PAWA (Pan-American Wrestling Association):** Funds travel to continental championships, coaching courses, equipment grants — flows through Federación Mexicana de Lucha
- **UWW Athlete Support:** Monthly stipends (USD $500–$1,500) for world-ranked athletes in developing nations

### World Athletics — Most Immediate Economic Value
- **Mexican road race prize money:** MXN $3,000–$100,000+ depending on event and placing
- **Sub-2:15 marathon (men) / sub-2:32 (women):** Earns MXN $100,000–$500,000/year from Mexican road racing alone
- Register on World Athletics (worldathletics.org) — free; creates permanent searchable results record
- NACAC (Mexico's regional body) provides competition travel funding through FMAA

### IOC Olympic Solidarity — The Global Ceiling
- **Olympic Scholarship for Athletes (OSA):** ~600 athletes globally; USD $1,500–$2,500/month for up to 3 years pre-Games
- **LA 2028 cycle is now active** — COM nominations for 2025–2028 are ongoing
- **Gateway:** National federation → COM (olimpiada.org.mx)
- Athletes currently 16–20 with national-level potential in Olympic sports are the target demographic

### IJF (Judo), IWF (Weightlifting), IBA/World Boxing, UCI (Cycling)
All provide development funding that flows through national federations:
- Equipment grants to clubs
- Competition travel for developing-nation athletes
- Coaching education
- Short-term training camp access

## SECTION 4 — International University Scholarships

### Japan — Most Realistic for Judo/Wrestling
- Top judo universities (Tokai, Kokushikan, Tenri, Nihon) recruit international judoka
- Package: tuition + dorm + ¥30,000–$80,000/month (~USD $200–$550)
- **MEXT Government Scholarship:** Full funding including ~¥117,000/month living allowance; apply through Japanese Embassy Mexico City
- Barrier: Pan-American level results required; Japanese language needed
- Age window: 18–22

### China — Best Package, Most Sport Coverage
- Beijing Sport University, Wuhan Sport University recruit wrestling, judo, weightlifting athletes
- **CSC (Chinese Government Scholarship):** Full tuition + dorm + CNY 2,500/month (~USD $350)
- Barrier: International competition results + federation nomination
- Sport universities often have international athlete tracks with Spanish support
- Age window: 18–24

### Spain — Zero Language Barrier
- **AECID Scholarships (Agencia Española de Cooperación):** Academic scholarships for Latin American students; combinable with sport at receiving university
- CAR Barcelona (Sant Cugat): Short-term training camp access through bilateral federation agreements; not a scholarship pathway for individuals
- Cost of living in Spain (especially Madrid/Barcelona) is the main barrier without full funding
- Most accessible European pathway due to language

### Russia/Eastern Europe — Diminished Post-2022
- Pre-2022 Russian sport universities (RSUPE) offered full wrestling/judo/boxing scholarships
- Post-2022 sanctions and isolation have significantly reduced this option
- **Not recommended as primary pathway**

### Korea, Germany, France, Canada, Australia — Lower Priority
- Language barriers, insufficient funding levels, or sport specialty mismatch reduce viability

## SECTION 5 — Professional Pathways

### Boxing — Highest Income Ceiling in Mexico

**Career earnings trajectory:**
| Stage | Fights | Purse per fight |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| Entry (4-round prelims) | 1–10 | MXN $3,000–$10,000 |
| Regional (6-8 round) | 10–20 | MXN $15,000–$40,000 |
| National/TV card | 20+ | MXN $50,000–$200,000+ |

**Optimal strategy:** Build amateur career with CONADE support → turn professional at 20–22 → manager/promoter signs at 8–12 fight record
- Major promoters: Zanfer, Miura Boxing, Golden Boy (US-based signing Mexicans)
- State boxing commission issues professional license at 18+ (minimum secondary school)
- Manager takes 30–33% of purses

**The Canelo Effect:** Massive international investment in Mexican boxing makes this the most commercially supported career pathway.

---

### MMA — Wrestling/Boxing Crossover

- **Combate Global** (Azteca TV): USD $3,500–$8,000 per televised fight for contracted fighters; actively scouts Mexico
- **UFC pathway:** Regional MMA → Combate → Dana White Contender Series → UFC
- Elite wrestler or boxer aged 18–22 with MMA crossover skills is exactly the demographic UFC development scouts seek
- Brandon Moreno (UFC world champion) and Yair Rodríguez are the proof-of-concept

---

### Distance Running — Semi-Professional Reality

- Elite Mexican road running circuit provides genuine semi-pro income
- Sub-2:15 marathon level: MXN $100,000–$500,000/year from prize money
- Highland Nahua altitude physiology is a structural advantage to reach these times faster
- The Rarámuri media narrative demonstrates that indigenous Mexican distance runners attract global interest — a Nahua runner replicates this story

---

### Lucha Libre — Separate Career, Not a Complement

- CMLL / AAA are entertainment wrestling; unrelated to competitive Olympic wrestling
- Entry: CMLL academy ~MXN $500–$1,000/month tuition; debut age 17–19
- Earnings: MXN $500–$2,000/show initially; MXN $10,000–$50,000+ for established stars
- **Trade-off:** Choosing Lucha Libre ends Olympic wrestling ambitions. It's a different career, not an addition.

## SECTION 6 — Optimal Age-Stage Roadmap

### Ages 12–15 — Foundation
1. Enroll in school → Activate Beca Bienestar immediately
2. Access INPI albergue support if relocating to city for school + sport
3. Train at IMSS Centro Deportivo (free/near-free, coach on staff)
4. Enter Juegos Deportivos Municipales and Estatales
5. Register in CONADEPI Indigenous Games (visibility + institutional contact)

### Ages 15–18 — Development
1. Compete at IDERV/CODEH level → Apply for state sport beca
2. Register with national sport federation
3. Target Juegos Deportivos Nacionales (CONADE) — the key national showcase
4. Universiada Nacional if enrolled in university/prep
5. Begin building World Athletics results profile (distance runners)

### Ages 18–22 — High Performance / Transition
1. **University track:** UV or UAEH enrollment → stack Bienestar + athletic + state beca (MXN $5,400–$9,400/month)
2. **Military track:** SEDENA Soldado Deportista → MXN $9,500–$12,000/month + CISM international competition
3. CONADE SINADE registration → target Nivel I–II beca
4. Pursue COM Olympic Solidarity nomination for LA 2028 cycle
5. Begin Pan-American Youth/U-23 Championship competition
6. **Boxing:** Evaluate professional license at 20+ while building amateur record
7. **Wrestling:** Target UWW Academy (Tashkent) nomination through federation
8. **Distance Running:** Enter prize-paying road races; plan marathon debut at 22–24
