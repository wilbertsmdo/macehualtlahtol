# Cross-Language Word-Formation Morpheme Mapping Table
## English · Spanish · Modern Huasteca Nahuatl (ACK orthography — Modern Huasteca Nahuatl, Chicontepec, Veracruz)

**Purpose:** Reference for the Nahuatl neologism generator. For each functional category: identify the English/Spanish morpheme, find the Nahuatl functional equivalent, assess productivity in Modern Huasteca Nahuatl, and supply an actionable generator rule.

**Dialect:** Modern Huasteca Nahuatl (Eastern Huastecan / Chicontepec variety), as documented by IDIEZ (Instituto de Docencia e Investigación Etnológica de Zacatecas) and Sullivan 2016 (Tlahtolxitlauhcayotl Chicontepec).

**Key sources:** Andrews 2003 (Classical morphology), Launey 2011 (Introduction to Classical Nahuatl), Sullivan/IDIEZ 2016, Gruda et al. 2023 (Lexical creativity in modern Nahuatl), Canger & Dakin 1985, Tuggy 1979 (Huasteca morphology), Brockway 1979 (Huastecan grammar).

**Orthography note:** All Nahuatl forms use ACK (Andrews-Campbell-Karttunen) orthography (k not qu/c, w not hu, s not z/c before e/i, ts not tz). Classical orthography equivalents given in parentheses where helpful.

## APPENDIX: GENERATOR QUICK-DECISION TREE

When coining a neologism for an English/Spanish term:

```
1. What word class is the target concept?
   ├── PERSON/AGENT (doer, practitioner, expert)
   │   └── → verb + -ni  [Rule A1, A2, E25]
   ├── THING/DEVICE/PRODUCT (instrument, result, artifact)
   │   ├── Machine/electronic → tepoz- + verb + -ni  [Rule A2]
   │   └── Abstract result → verb + -(l)li  [Rule A3]
   ├── PLACE/INSTITUTION (where X occurs)
   │   └── → verb + -yan / -loyan  [Rule A4]
   ├── PROCESS/ACT (the doing of X)
   │   └── → verb + -liztli  [Rule A5]
   ├── QUALITY/SYSTEM/-NESS/-ISM
   │   └── → noun/adj + -yotl  [Rule A6, A7]
   └── MODIFIER (pertaining to X, characterized by X)
       └── → noun-noun compound or [X]-yoh  [Rule B10]

2. Does the concept involve a POLARITY or DEGREE?
   ├── Negative/without → a- + concept  [Rule B11, D23]
   ├── Very/hyper → cenca + derived noun  [Rule C15]
   ├── Small/micro → -tontli / achi +  [Rule C16, A8]
   ├── Large/macro → huey- +  [Rule A9, C15]
   └── Many/multi → miec- +  [Rule C17, E34]

3. Does the concept involve SELF or CAUSATION?
   ├── Self/auto → mo- + verb + -liztli  [Rule B13, E35]
   └── Make/cause to X → verb + -tia/-ltia  [Rule B12]

4. Is it a GREEK/LATIN SCIENTIFIC stem?
   ├── Study of → tla-[domain]-iximatiliztli  [Rule E24]
   ├── Practitioner → tla-[domain]-iximatini  [Rule E25]
   ├── Measurement → tepoz-[domain]-pohuani  [Rule E26]
   ├── Viewing → tepoz-[domain]-ittoni  [Rule E30]
   ├── Government by → [group]-tlahtocayotl  [Rule E33]
   └── Writing/recording → [domain]-ihkuiliztli  [Rule E29]
```
